# Data license and attribution

The result summaries in `results/` and the concept figure are released under the Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 International license: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.en

This repository contains no audio. The scripts download or read the following corpora, each under its own terms.

| Corpus | Use in this work | Terms |
|---|---|---|
| JSUT BASIC5000 and jsut-label | native training speech, manual phonemes, native duration categories | audio for research and non-commercial use, no redistribution; labels CC BY-SA 4.0 |
| Common Voice 17.0, Japanese | native training and validation speech | CC0 1.0 |
| FLEURS, ja_jp | native training speech | CC BY 4.0 |
| UME-JRF (DOI 10.32130/src.UME-JRF) | rated learner speech for evaluation | research use only, available on application from the NII Informatics Research Data Repository, no redistribution |

Simulated training data can be regenerated with the scripts. Simulations built only from Common Voice and FLEURS
inherit the terms of those corpora.
